Join Los Angeles Beyond Celiac Ambassador Talia Abrahamson for a celiac-safe taco buffet from Hugo's Tacos, hosted in the private dining room at Hugo's West Hollywood, where we're capping the night off with gluten-free churros. It's a fun evening where the Los Angeles gluten-free community can enjoy a meal with confidence and get to know each other.

Sunday, August 23 6:30 – 8:30 PM Hugo's West Hollywood

A Personal Welcome

This dinner is about more than tacos and churros—though I won't complain if they're the highlight of your night. I love creating opportunities to bring people together, and this is one of them. Whether you've been to Hugo's West Hollywood or Hugo's Tacos many times before or this is your first visit, I hope this evening feels like a new experience.

My goal is for everyone to enjoy great food, reconnect with familiar faces, meet someone new or leave with a few memorable conversations. I put together this page to answer any questions you might have before the dinner, including menu details and a complete ingredient and allergen guide.

Thanks for considering my invitation. I can't wait to share the evening with all of you.

— Talia Abrahamson
Los Angeles Beyond Celiac Ambassador

About this event

Hugo's Tacos is bringing its tacos into the private dining room at Hugo's West Hollywood, its sister restaurant, exclusively for our group. Move down the buffet line and build your plate from a spread of celiac-safe fillings, salsas and toppings, with vegan options available. Dessert is gluten-free and vegan churros!

Why Hugo's Tacos?
Hugo's Tacos and Hugo's West Hollywood have put real care into building a buffet the whole group can trust, with celiac-safe prep for every station.
